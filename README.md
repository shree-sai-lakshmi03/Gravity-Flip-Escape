# 🌀 GRAVITY FLIP ESCAPE

> **Flip gravity. Dodge danger. Find your way out.**

A fast-paced 2D escape game where gravity isn't fixed, the floor isn't always the floor, and one wrong move can send you straight into trouble.

---

## 🎮 THE IDEA

**Gravity Flip Escape** puts you in control of a character trapped in a dangerous environment.

Your goal is simple:

### **🏃 Move → 🔄 Flip → ⚡ Survive → 🚪 Escape**

Use gravity to your advantage, navigate obstacles, avoid hazards, and reach the exit.

But there's a catch...

**The world can turn upside down at any moment.**

---

## ✨ FEATURES

* 🌀 **Gravity Flip Mechanic**

  * Switch between normal and inverted gravity to navigate the level.

* 🧩 **Obstacle-Based Gameplay**

  * Time your movements carefully and find the safest route.

* ⚡ **Fast-Paced Challenges**

  * React quickly. Gravity waits for absolutely nobody.

* 🎯 **Simple Controls, Tricky Levels**

  * Easy to understand, harder to master.

* 🚪 **Escape-Based Objective**

  * Reach the exit without getting caught by the hazards.

---

## 🕹️ CONTROLS

| Action       | Key       |
| ------------ | --------- |
| Move Left    | `A` / `←` |
| Move Right   | `D` / `→` |
| Flip Gravity | `Space`   |
| Restart      | `R`       |

> *Controls may change as development continues.*

---

## 🧠 HOW TO PLAY

### 01 · RUN

Move through the level and look for a safe path.

### 02 · FLIP

When the path ahead becomes impossible, **flip gravity**.

### 03 · ADAPT

Platforms that were once above you are now below you.

### 04 · ESCAPE

Make it through the hazards and reach the exit.
